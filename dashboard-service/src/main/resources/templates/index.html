Data transparency — what is real and what is simulated

  • SEED DATA Catalogue, BigBasket prices & ratings. Scraped from the public bigbasket.com/pb/go-zero/ pages (robots.txt respected, 1.5 s between requests, capped at 25 products). The live BigBasket scrape was blocked or unavailable, so a bundled illustrative seed dataset is shown. These are not Go Zero's actual numbers.
  • SEED DATA Customer reviews. Scraped from BigBasket product pages. BigBasket loads reviews dynamically, so review text comes (fully or partly) from the seed dataset. Each review carries its own isLiveData flag.
  • SIMULATED Blinkit, Zepto & Instamart price and stock. Generated by reconciliation-service: these platforms have no public catalogue and need seller-API access. Anomalies (price gaps, stock-outs, missing listings) are deliberately injected to exercise the alert rules. Only the BigBasket reference price comes from ingestion-service.
  • COMPUTED Sentiment & complaint categories. Produced in-house by a ~200-term lexicon scorer with negation handling. No external ML API.
